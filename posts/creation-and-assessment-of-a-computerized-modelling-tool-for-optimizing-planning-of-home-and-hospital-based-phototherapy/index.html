Creation and assessment of a computerized modelling tool for optimizing planning of home and hospital-based phototherapy

  • Authors: P. Arzpayma, H. Jones, Paul Harper, Vincent Knight, D. Shipley, C. Edwards, A. Anstey.
  • Date: 2017
  • Details: British Journal of Dermatology, 176(5), pp. 1390--1391
  • Keywords: phototherapy, dermatology, healthcare modelling

This is a short communication reporting a computerised planning tool for scheduling home- and hospital-based phototherapy, published as a letter in the British Journal of Dermatology; no abstract was published alongside it.
