Cytuno, from the Welsh for 'to agree', applies evolutionary game theory to
international cooperation, including trade and climate agreements. The award
would fund reciprocal research visits between Cardiff and the University of
Amsterdam, together with a feasibility study scoping a joint European Research
Council Synergy Grant bid.

The application deepens an established collaboration with Sebastian Krapohl,
with whom we have a paper under review applying evolutionary game theory to
international trade, and with whom we jointly supervise a doctoral student
working on climate agreements. It also extends the collaboration to the Vrije
Universiteit Brussel in Flanders, a Welsh Government priority region.

The application was submitted through Cardiff University's research and
innovation office. A decision is expected by 1 November 2026, in time to book
travel for the January visit.
